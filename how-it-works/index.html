Workflow

From a paragraph to a priced shot.

Narrator is the desk where a brief becomes an editable shot list. It is not a promise of a finished film with dialogue, music, and a cut.

01

Write the brief

One paragraph is enough. Narrator drafts scenes and shots with size, angle, movement, and a five second duration you can edit.

02

Lock the references

Add characters, places, and props. Each can hold up to eight images. Those references are named in the prompt for every shot they belong to.

03

Approve the price

A still, a 480p draft, and a 720p finish each show a credit price before the job starts. Credits are held, then captured or returned.

What happens after you approve

The job is queued. Credits are reserved in one database transaction. A background worker calls the configured image or video provider, or a local sample when no key is set. On success the smaller of the quote and the settled provider bill is captured. On failure the hold is returned.

You can download each file, download a zip, or build a silent cut when ffmpeg is installed. Drive, Dropbox, and social publishing are optional, Maker and Studio only, and they never change the credit price.

Rights follow the provider that made the file. Sample files in mock mode are for trying the desk.
