What the credits buy

One still: 0.8 credits ($0.04). A reference-capable frame at the catalog still price.

5 second draft: 12.9 credits ($0.645). 480p video. This is the default generate button.

5 second finish: 33.8 credits ($1.69). 720p video. You choose this on purpose. It is a higher quote.

480p is the default. 720p is a separate quote you choose on the shot. There is no auto-refill.

Pricing questions

When do monthly credits expire?

Plan credits expire at the end of the paid period. A failed renewal grants nothing. Unused plan credits do not roll into the next month.

Do packs expire?

No. Pack credits stay until you spend them. You need a paid plan to buy a pack. The 1,000 credit pack is locked for the first 24 hours of a new account.

Which credits are spent first?

Plan credits go first, then other credits that expire soonest, then packs. That way a monthly grant is used before a pack sits behind it.

What if a job fails?

The credit hold is returned. A finished file you simply dislike stays charged. Make another take at a new quoted price.

Can I get a cash refund?

Duplicate or mistaken charges can be refunded within 7 days, and only for credits that are still unspent. Those credits are removed in the same step. Cancel keeps the plan until the period ends.

Who owns the pictures?

Rights follow the provider that made the file. Narrator does not grant a separate commercial-use license. Mock files are samples for trying the product.
